Privacy Policy

Cloud Quran · last updated 3 October 2026

Cloud Quran is a free Quran reader with no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It is published by Noble Achievements LLC. Its source code is public at github.com/Devoxer/cloud-quran, so everything below can be checked against the code.

Reading needs no account and stays on your device

The Quran text, translations you download, your bookmarks and your place in the Quran live on your device. The app works offline. You never have to sign in.

What is synced, and when

To carry your place between devices, the app keeps a small record on our server, linked to an account. On first launch it creates an anonymous account with no name or email. Only these are synced:

  • your reading position (surah, ayah, page and reading mode);
  • your bookmarks;
  • your listening position and chosen voice;
  • your preferences (theme, text size, reading mode, translation, playback speed).

What you read reveals religious practice, so we treat it as special-category data. You can turn syncing off in Settings › Your Data at any time; this device then stops sending and fetching, and nothing is deleted.

Signing in (optional)

If you sign in with email, Google or Apple, we store the account's name, email address and the sign-in method, plus a session record (including the IP address and device user agent at sign-in) so you stay signed in. Your sign-in provider learns that you signed in to Cloud Quran, not what you read. Email sign-in sends you a one-time code.

Content downloads

Recitation and narration audio, mushaf page fonts and translation packs are downloaded from our own content server, cdn.nobleachievements.com. Like any web server it receives your IP address with each request. The app does not contact the content's original publishers while you read.

Crash reports (off unless you turn them on)

If you turn on Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports, error reports are sent to Sentry with personal data, authorization headers and credentials removed. It is off by default.

Who processes the data

  • Cloudflare — hosts the server, the synced data, sign-in email delivery and the content server.
  • Google or Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Sentry — only if you turn on crash reports.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Your controls

  • Export — Settings › Your Data › Export my data gives you a JSON file of everything synced.
  • Delete synced data — empties everything synced; your account stays.
  • Delete account — removes the account, its synced data and its sign-in methods. You keep reading as a guest.

Synced data is kept until you delete it or the account. Sign-in sessions expire on their own.

Children

Cloud Quran collects no more from children than from anyone else, and none of it is used for advertising or profiling.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published here and in the public repository's history. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Devoxer/cloud-quran/issues, or use Settings › Send Feedback in the app.
